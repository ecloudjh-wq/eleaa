E CLOUD AI LANGUAGE MODEL

A proprietary language model that understands
how children speak and learn

ELM is E Cloud AI’s proprietary language model, designed around the speech, sentence construction, errors, and learning-response patterns of non-native children ages 5–13.
It understands textbooks, learner levels, and learning objectives, then connects questions, hints, corrections, and retries based on each learner’s response to deliver context-appropriate interaction.

Child-Specific Understanding

Understands children's pronunciation, sentence construction, errors, and response patterns

Curriculum Grounding

Builds dialogue around textbooks, levels, and learning objectives

Learning-Aware Dialogue

Provides questions, hints, and corrections based on learning stage and response flow

ASSESSMENT & FEEDBACK ENGINE

ELEA analyzes learners’ speaking and writing outcomes using criteria aligned with their learning stage and objectives.
Learners receive targeted feedback and repeated practice, while teachers and operators receive learning results and reports.
